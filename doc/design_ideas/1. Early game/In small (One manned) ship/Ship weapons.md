# Ship weapons

## Energy

### Laser

Long range low energy efficiency weapon. DPS depends on `Power distributor`.
Useless in most cases due to low efficiency. Mostly used to destroy satelites, mines, aproaching projectiles, or people in space suites.

### Shocker

Extremly short range (Your ship need touch the enemy ship) high efficiency non lethal weapon.
Mostly used by pirats. Fries elecronic on the target ship, requiring repair afterwards. Need charging right after use. Discharge triggers on impact. Recharging speed depends on `Power distributor`

## Kinetic

### Projectile types

* Rod - has extremely high penetration. Leaves small through-and-through hole in enemy ship
* Explosive - detonates on impact or in certain range to enemy ship. Has wide, but not deep, explosion range
* Schrapnel - makes a lot of tiny holes.  Kills personel and destroys sensitive equipment with very high chance. Has trouble penetrating armor. It is harder for enemy ship to avade a cloud of schrapnel

### Tragectory Сorrection

* noting - Projectile doesn't correct its tragectory. Such weapons usualy rely on short use range, high speed or sheer amount, to be able to hit the target.
* thruster - Currect its tragectory using internal fuel/energy. Stops correcting when when it is depleated. Can have visual or infrared tracking (See Tracking)

### Tracking

* none - for weapons without tragectory correction
* visual - relies on visual spectr to track the enemy. has high accuracy deterioration with range (aka low range). Can not see, when  the enemy is behind the obstacle. Can not see in shadow
* infrared - high range. Can not see, when the enemy is behind the obstacle. Can not see with any celestial body in the background.

### Initial acceleration 

* noting - Just releases the projectile. Relies on the ship acceleration.
* firearm - Plain old gun powder
* railgun - By release of an annormous electric charge. Needs time to charge right before firing. Can not abort after charging is started. Uses lots of energy. Charging time depends on `Power distributor`
* coilgun - By accelerating a projectile in series of magnet coils. Fires immediatly on command but needs to be charged beforehand. Uses some energy. Speed of fire or/and speed of projectile depends on `Power distributor`

### Later additional acceleration

* nothing - don't accelerate after launched
* missle - accelerates like a missile. Can be usefull when trying to catch fast accelerating enemy. Very expensive, used only by military.
