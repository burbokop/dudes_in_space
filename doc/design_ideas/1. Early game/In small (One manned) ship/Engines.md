# Engines

## Compressed gas

Just shoots compressed gas out of its nozzle. Low efficiency, low thrust, wery cheap, wery small, wery low mass. Mostly used as manuevering thrusters for ships, space suites and tracking projectiles

## Chemical

* Liquid fuel - high thrust, reusable, requires fuel and oxidizer, low efficiency. Mostly used in fights.
* Solid fuel - huge thrust, not reusable, low efficiency. Can not be stopped when started. Mostly used for missiles, bording pods or other one use vessels. Also can be used for decoupling large structures and in sertain emergency cases.

## Nuclear

* NTP (Nuclear Thermal Propulsion) - Low thrust, medium efficiency. Requres gas (hydrogen) and nuclear fuel (uranium, plutonium). Generates some amount of electricity as sideeffect. Mostly used for long distance trevels.
* NDP (Neclear Detonation Propulsion) - Generates thrust by ejecting pallets with nuclear fuel and detonatin behind space craft. Medium thrust, medium (bigger than NTP) efficiency. Can be lethal to other vessels behind. Dengerous to work with. Expensive both engine and fuel. Has unusually high mass. Banned in some factions due to ecologocal concerns. Mostly used for extralong distance trevels.

## Ion

* NEP (Nuclear Electric Propulsion) - Accelerates ions by ellectric charge generated from heat of neclear reaction. Tiny thrust, high efficiency, Requres gas (hydrogen) and nuclear fuel (uranium, plutonium). Generates some amount of electricity as sideeffect. Mostly used in sattelites and small ships. 
* Solar powered ion engine - Accelerates ions by ellectric charge generated by sollar panel. Generates some amount of electricity as sideeffect. Mostly used in sattelites and small ships.

## Fusion

Fusion engines is an experimental technology in the world of the game. Player can find them only on specific science ships or prototype ships. Can not be bought. Can be reserched through special plot line requiring a huge amount of finance and specific relations with specific factions or individuals. Can be stollen from specific science facilities.

* Electromagnetic pulse ion engine -  Uses two simetrical field reverse configurations to create plasma rings, then collides them. Generated during collision electromagnetic field accelerates ions directly and shoots them out of the nozzle. Small sized, high efficiency, high mass, low thrust engine. Requires gas (hydrogen), d-he3 fuel and lots of energy. Generates some byproducts that can be used instead of hydrogen in the nozzle.

* Laser fusion engine - Uses lasers to heat a pallet of termonuclear fuel. Explosion generates thrust directly. Small sized, high efficiency, high mass, low thrust engine. Requires gas (hydrogen), d-t fuel and lots of energy. Can be dengerous to use.

* Tokamak ion engine - Generates electric energy from heat of fusion reaction. This electric energy is used to power ion engine. Ennormous mass, size, and efficiency, low thrust engine. Requres gas (hydrogen), d-t fuel, lots of energy but starts to generate some after some time of sustained uninterrupted operation. Generates some byproducts that can be used instead of hydrogen in the nozzle.

## Matter-Antimeter

Even more rere then fusion cuz it not only needs a lot of research done and a plot line that is even harder to stumble upon, but cuz fuel for it is so costly that it is completely impractical to find a use for it except just for fun. Uses M-A (Matter-Antimeter) mix stored in spetial container that divides a M and A using magnetic fields and eats a lot of energy. If sufficient energy supply is not provided, Container is gona explode. The fuel also leaks out over time so you need to produce even more of it, and producing small amount (below certain point) is gona leak out completely even before you start the engine. So yeh, completely impractical to use it as a propustion source. At least you can make a big boom with it.

Some factions might go to war with you if you aquire such technology, cuz noone wants a big boom.

## Crimson engine

If u take certain pills bought on black market, the engine of your ship may transform into "Crimson engine" which allows you to drive very fast without using any fuel. You can event enter hyperspace. The sideeffect is that afterwards you may find your ship located at a random spot on the map and with empty fuel tank and your character having a funny fealing witch temporarly increases certain stats and decreases other. Also can activate certain plot lines or random events. For example you may find your ship missing some crutial parts or even don't find it at all, floating in a space suite or being sold to slavery by some pirats. Certainly something you may want to do if you want some kind of andvanture. Can also have effect of the world. For example if your ship was stollen by pirats, you may find it later with some elaborate mods, plundered or with some other story to tell you about what it went through.
